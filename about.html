About the school

About Channawar's e Vidya Mandir

Run by Aakar Education Society (Reg. No. MH/257/10), Channawar's e Vidya Mandir is a premier CBSE-affiliated co-educational school in Wardha. We combine a rigorous academic foundation with active sports, performing arts, and foundational values to prepare students for life, not just examinations.

Located on an expansive, lush campus near Vyankatesh Polytechnic on Arvi Road, our school provides a safe, joyful environment where curiosity is celebrated and every student receives personalized attention.

Our Mission, Vision & Values

To provide an inclusive, joyful and rigorous education that empowers every student to become a confident, compassionate and capable citizen.
